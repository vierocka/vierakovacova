# Publications

Full, current list with citations: [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Tz7QKnAAAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4581-4254). Author lists are shortened; my name is in bold, and each entry says what I did.

## Peer-reviewed

- Szobi et al., incl. **Kovacova** (2023) Vivid COVID-19 LAMP is an ultrasensitive, quadruplexed test using LNA-modified primers and a zinc ion and 5-Br-PAPS colorimetric detection system. *Communications Biology* 6:233. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9979146/)

  **My part:** primer design and the in-silico sequence analyses required for US and Canadian regulatory submissions.

- Radvanszka, Paul, Hajdu, Boršová, **Kovacova** et al. (2022) Sequential development of several RT-qPCR tests using LNA nucleotides and dual probe technology to differentiate SARS-CoV-2 from influenza A and B. *Microbial Biotechnology* 15(7):1995–2021.

  **My part:** primer design and in-silico sequence checks.

- Power, Pinheiro, Pompei, **Kovacova** et al. (2021) Adaptive evolution of hybrid bacteria by horizontal gene transfer. *PNAS* 118:e2007873118. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7958396/)

  **My part:** all bioinformatics, from assembling the hybrid genomes to calling point mutations, plus the transcriptomics (rlog and ComBat normalization, GO enrichment). A read-mapping benchmark under genomic divergence set the alignment parameters ([CRC 1310 page](CRC-1310-Central-Bioinformatics)).

- He, Steige, **Kovacova** et al. (2021) Cis-regulatory evolution spotlights species differences in the adaptive potential of gene expression plasticity. *Nature Communications* 12:3376. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-23558-2)

  **My part:** reference-guided de novo assembly for *Arabidopsis halleri*, orthogroups, and alignments compared across several tools to quantify pairwise sequence differences.

- Boršová, Paul, **Kovacova** et al. (2021) Surveillance of SARS-CoV-2 lineage B.1.1.7 in Slovakia using a novel, multiplexed RT-qPCR assay. *Scientific Reports* 11:20494. Shared first authorship. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8516907/)

  **My part:** all bioinformatics and the primer design. That meant large SARS-CoV-2 alignments, cross-checks against the human transcriptome and the closest related pathogens, and screening for hairpins and self-structures that would compromise the PCR.

- Baránek, **Kovacova** et al. (2021) Epigenetic modulating chemicals significantly affect the virulence and genetic characteristics of the bacterial plant pathogen *Xanthomonas campestris* pv. *campestris*. *Genes*.

  **My part:** transcriptome analysis, sequence parsing with regular expressions, and the basic statistics.

- Mahdi et al., incl. **Kovacova** (2020) Discovery of a family of mixed lineage kinase domain-like proteins in plants and their role in innate immune signaling. *Cell Host & Microbe* 28:813–824. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33053377/)

  **My part:** a smaller contribution, the phylogenetics of the plant MLKL family, reusing the orthogroups built for the *Plant Cell* study.

- Lapin, **Kovacova**, Sun et al. (2019) A coevolved EDS1–SAG101–NRG1 module mediates cell death signaling by TIR-domain immune receptors. *The Plant Cell* 31:2430–2455. Shared first authorship. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31311833/)

  **My part:** I built the orthogroup resource (OrthoMCL) and removed false-positive members by filtering on functional domains. On that basis came the phylogenies and the presence/absence profiles of the module's proteins across species. I also ran dN/dS models (PAML) contrasting how surface and pocket residues evolve.

- Panfilio, Vargas Jentzsch, Benoit et al., incl. **Kovacova** (2019) Molecular evolutionary trends and feeding ecology diversification in the Hemiptera, anchored by the milkweed bug genome. *Genome Biology* 20:64. [Open access](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/115066)

  **My part:** polishing the de novo genome assembly with PacBio and short reads, protein-domain prediction (HMMER), and orthogroup-based gene annotation with OrthoMCL, as in the *Plant Cell* study.

- Balounova et al., incl. **Kovacova** (2019) Evolution of sex determination and heterogamety changes in section *Otites* of the genus *Silene*. *Scientific Reports* 9:1045.

  **My part:** de novo assemblies, data filtering, CDS prediction and annotation, the computational basis of the study.

- Nourmohammad, Rambeau, Held, **Kovacova**, Berg, Lässig (2017) Adaptive evolution of gene expression in *Drosophila*. *Cell Reports* 20:1385–1395. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28793262/)

  **My part:** RNA-seq processing, and a comparison of normalization methods for their effect on data structure across species, tissues and developmental stages, as the basis for the cross-species comparison. More data were processed and tested than the final paper used.

- Fruciano, Franchini, **Kovacova**, Elmer, Henning, Meyer (2016) Genetic linkage of distinct adaptive traits in sympatrically speciating crater lake cichlid fish. *Nature Communications* 7:12736. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms12736)

  **My part:** de novo assemblies for five fish, extensive data filtering and CDS prediction, then phylogenetics, pairwise alignments and selection analyses (PAML).

- **Kovacova**, Zluvova, Janousek, Talianova, Vyskot (2014) The evolutionary fate of the horizontally transferred agrobacterial mikimopine synthase gene in the genera *Nicotiana* and *Linaria*. *PLoS ONE* 9:e113872. First author. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4242671/)

  **My part:** the whole study, from the wet lab to the evolutionary analyses, including evidence of small-RNA-mediated regulation of the transferred gene.

- Kubat, Zluvova, Vogel, **Kovacova** et al. (2014) Possible mechanisms responsible for absence of a retrotransposon family on a plant Y chromosome. *New Phytologist* 202:662–678.

  **My part:** wet-lab detection of 5-methylcytosine by bisulfite sequencing, processing of the sequencing reads, and the statistical tests.

- Baloun, Nevrtalova, **Kovacova** et al. (2014) Characterization of the HMA7 gene and transcriptomic analysis of candidate genes for copper tolerance in two *Silene vulgaris* ecotypes. *Journal of Plant Physiology* 171:1188–1196.

  **My part:** the statistical tests.

- Slancarova et al., incl. **Kovacova** (2013) Evolution of sex determination systems with heterogametic males and females in *Silene*. *Evolution* 67:3669–3677.

  **My part:** de novo assemblies, data filtering, CDS prediction and annotation, the computational basis of the study.

- **Kovacova**, Janousek (2012) Bisprimer: a program for the design of primers for bisulfite-based genomic sequencing of both plant and mammalian DNA samples. *Journal of Heredity* 103:308–312. First author. [Record](https://muni.cz/en/research/publications/970036)

  **My part:** the whole tool. I learned the bisulfite protocol at the bench, then designed and coded BisPrimer around what my wet-lab colleagues needed for plant and mammalian DNA.
