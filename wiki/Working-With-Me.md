# Working with me

> Bring a question, a dataset or a half-finished analysis. I start with what the data can support, then propose the cheapest test that settles the next decision.

## Problems I take on

**Can this old or public dataset still be used?**
I audit the read-level QC that already exists (trimming and mapping logs, rRNA fraction, replicate structure) and return a keep / correct / drop decision per sample, before anyone pays for re-sequencing. Example: an older cell-line library left out instead of averaged in ([QC and retrospective validation](QC-and-Retrospective-Validation)).

**Are these DE genes real?**
I check batch structure, sensitivity to normalization, effect-size rules, degradation imbalance between groups, and the mappability of the genes in question. Example: identical replicates from one batch, split 3 vs 3, give 689 "DE" genes ([Froussios2019_batch_effect_series](https://github.com/vierocka/Froussios2019_batch_effect_series)).

**Will this biomarker panel hold up?**
I look for the smallest sufficient panel, estimate optimism-corrected performance, lock the model before external validation, and stress-test it for assay noise, selection bias and sex/age confounding ([Biomarkers](Biomarkers)).

**Can expression of immune genes or gene families be trusted?**
I measure transcript uniqueness, quantify HLA in an allele-aware way, and list the loci that need long reads ([Mappability and immunopeptidomics](Mappability-and-Immunopeptidomics)).

**A non-model organism without a good reference?**
De novo genome or transcriptome assembly and polishing, orthogroup-based annotation, phylogenetics and dN/dS selection analyses ([Comparative genomics and plant immunity](Comparative-Genomics-and-Plant-Immunity)).

**Data scattered across folders and spreadsheets?**
A lean database with metadata rules, submission and release logic and a web front end, built quickly with guided AI coding ([Data infrastructure](Data-Infrastructure)).

**A model that needs clean data?**
Data preparation, benchmarks such as read mapping under divergence, and model fitting together with theory groups ([CRC 1310 central bioinformatics](CRC-1310-Central-Bioinformatics)).
